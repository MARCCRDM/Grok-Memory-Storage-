# Gladys Niche Targets: Camera & Recording-Heavy Medical Practices

Practices whose workflow already involves cameras, imaging, or recording equipment. These are the easiest entry points for Gladys because the hardware and consent infrastructure exist — Gladys only needs to license and de-identify what's already being captured.

## Tier 1: Native Video Capture (Highest Value)

### Gastroenterology
- Colonoscopy and endoscopy generate continuous video of every procedure
- Longitudinal records across years of screening
- High procedural volume, standardized capture
- Buyers want procedural video for surgical AI training

### Orthopedics
- Arthroscopy produces high-definition video of joint procedures
- Sports medicine and joint replacement documentation
- Video is core to surgical training datasets

### Cardiology
- Echocardiograms, cardiac cath lab fluoroscopy and video
- Stress test recordings, Holter monitor data
- Multimodal: imaging plus waveform data

### Urology
- Cystoscopy video, urodynamic studies
- Prostate procedure documentation

### OB/GYN
- Ultrasound video (routine and 4D), colposcopy
- Fetal monitoring tracings
- High-volume prenatal imaging

### ENT (Otolaryngology)
- Laryngoscopy, nasal endoscopy, otoscopy video
- Sinus surgery video
- Voice and swallowing studies

### Pulmonology
- Bronchoscopy video
- Sleep study polysomnography recordings

### General & Specialty Surgery
- Laparoscopic and robotic surgery video (da Vinci, etc.)
- Intraoperative recording is increasingly standard
- Neurosurgery uses intraoperative video and microscope feeds

### Ophthalmology
- OCT scans, fundus photography, slit lamp video
- Corneal topography, visual field tests
- Surgical microscope video for cataract and retinal procedures

## Tier 2: Photo & Imaging Capture (Strong Secondary)

### Dermatology
- Clinical photos and dermoscopy on nearly every visit
- Skin cancer screening archives, longitudinal mole tracking
- Highest photo volume per patient of any specialty

### Plastic & Cosmetic Surgery
- Before/after photo documentation is contractual
- Procedure photos, 3D imaging (Vectra)
- Patients already consent to photo use for marketing

### Wound Care & Podiatry
- Serial wound photography for healing tracking
- Diabetic foot imaging, vascular studies

### Pathology
- Digital pathology whole-slide imaging
- Biopsy slide archives

## Tier 3: Audio & Encounter Recording (Ambient Scribe Market)

### Primary Care (Family Medicine, Internal Medicine)
- Ambient AI scribes (Abridge, Freed, Suki, DAX) already record visits
- 29% of US physicians use them; 45% of private practices have tried
- Consent workflow already solved
- Highest patient volume, longitudinal notes

### Neurology
- Led specialty adoption of ambient scribes in Doximity survey
- Complex visit documentation, cognitive assessments

### Oncology
- DeepScribe claims 90% of community oncology centers
- Dense visit notes, treatment histories, longitudinal records
- Specialty-tuned documentation means richer free-text data

### Behavioral Health / Psychiatry
- Session recordings with consent
- Therapy transcripts are high-value conversational data
- Growing telehealth archive

### Telehealth Platforms
- Consented visit recordings and transcripts
- Mercor pays $100K–$900K for digital health archives
- Multilingual visits command premiums

## Why These Niches Fit Gladys

1. **Hardware exists** — cameras, endoscopes, ultrasound, and ambient mics are already in the room
2. **Consent exists** — patients already sign for procedure recording or ambient scribing
3. **Volume exists** — these specialties generate continuous, structured capture
4. **De-identification is tractable** — PHI stripping from video/audio is a solved pipeline problem
5. **Buyers exist** — AI labs and medical AI companies pay premium rates for procedural and encounter data

## Target Profile for Outreach

- Solo or small independent practices (2–10 clinicians) underserved by enterprise tools
- US-based, EHR-connected (Epic, athenahealth, eClinicalWorks)
- 12+ months of historical records per patient
- Free-text physician notes, not just structured fields
- Physician-owned (not hospital-system-owned) for faster decision-making
