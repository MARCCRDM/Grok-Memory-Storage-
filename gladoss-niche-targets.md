# Gladoss Niche Targets

Target audience and market positioning for Gladoss.

- Maturing players aging out of brainrot-style games who want a more serious smart home experience

- Privacy-conscious homeowners who avoid big-tech cloud ecosystems

- DIY tinkerers already comfortable with Zigbee, TP-Link, and self-hosted setups

- Early adopters who value open-source and local-first automation
