# Gladoss Client Targeting

Goal: onboard independent physicians and small medical practices as data suppliers for de-identified clinical datasets.

## Ideal Client Profile
- Independent, physician-owned practice (not hospital-affiliated)
- 10+ clinicians OR 2,500+ active patients with 12+ months of visit history
- EHR with rich free-text physician notes (Epic, athenahealth, eClinicalWorks, NextGen, Oracle Health, Elation)
- Primary care, internal medicine, family medicine, or high-value specialties (oncology, cardiology, neurology, rare disease)
- Longitudinal records: same patients across multiple years
- Payer mix including Medicare/Medicare Advantage (deep senior panels = chronic condition depth)

## What Gladoss Offers Them
- Revenue share on licensed de-identified data (market range: $80K-$820K per practice per Mercor benchmarks)
- HIPAA-compliant de-identification pipeline (Safe Harbor + Expert Determination)
- No change to their existing EHR or workflow
- They keep original records; Gladoss only receives de-identified extracts

## Outreach Approach
- Lead with money and staff time, not AI jargon
- Raise HIPAA/privacy proactively in first conversation
- Phone is highest-converting channel for small/solo practices
- Reference EHR type and patient volume early — those are the buyer's first questions
- Negative signals: heavy Medi-Cal panels, template-only notes, behavioral health (42 CFR Part 2), payer contract restrictions

## Qualification Questions
1. Which EHR do you use?
2. How many active patients, and how many years of history?
3. Do notes contain free-text narrative or mostly templates?
4. Any state or payer restrictions on data sharing?
5. Are you independent or hospital-owned?
